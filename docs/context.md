# Project Context: Mutual Fund FAQ Assistant

This document summarizes the core business context, objectives, boundaries, and reference specifications for the Mutual Fund FAQ Assistant. It serves as the single source of truth for the project's background and constraints.

---

## 1. Project Background & Business Goals

*   **Reference Product Context**: **Groww** (investment platform reference).
*   **Problem**: Retail investors frequently ask basic, objective questions about mutual fund details (expense ratios, lock-ins, exit loads). At the same time, customer support and content teams handle high volumes of repetitive, factual queries.
*   **Solution**: A lightweight Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)-based chatbot that answers objective, verifiable questions using official AMC, AMFI, or SEBI public data.
*   **Philosophy**: **Accuracy over Intelligence**. The chatbot must strictly report verifiable facts and avoid predicting, speculating, or advising.

---

## 2. Target Users

*   **Retail Investors**: Comparing fund specifics (e.g., exit loads, lock-in periods, fund manager tenure) to make informed decisions.
*   **Customer Support & Content Teams**: Internal staff seeking quick, verified info to resolve client queries.

### 3.2. Supported Queries (Facts-Only)
The assistant is designed to retrieve and answer the following factual points:
*   Expense ratios
*   Exit loads and redemption fees
*   Minimum SIP and lump sum investment amounts
*   ELSS lock-in periods
*   Riskometer risk classifications (e.g., High, Moderate)
*   Benchmark indices
*   Step-by-step procedures for downloading statements or capital gains reports
*   Fund management details (fund manager names, experience, tenure, and AUM/fund size)

### 3.3. Out of Scope & Refusals
The assistant must **refuse** to answer non-factual, subjective, or advisory queries, including:
*   Investment recommendations (*"Should I invest in X?"*)
*   Performance comparisons (*"Which fund is better?"* or *"Is X better than Y?"*)
*   Return projections or calculations (*"How much will I make?"*)
*   Questions about any scheme not listed in the 13 target schemes.

**Special Exception**: For performance-related queries, instead of giving returns or comparisons, the system must provide a direct citation link to the official fund factsheet.

---

## 4. Key Constraints & Compliance

### 4.1. Formatting Constraints
Every response generated by the system must meet these three conditions:
1.  **Length**: Maximum of **3 sentences**.
2.  **Citation**: Exactly **one citation link** to the official document source.
3.  **Footer**: Appended with: `Last updated from sources: <date>`.

### 4.2. Refusal Guidelines
When refusing a query, the assistant must:
*   Remain polite and professional.
*   Clearly state its limitation as a facts-only assistant.
*   Provide a relevant educational resource link (e.g., SEBI or AMFI guidance pages).

### 4.3. Privacy and Security Constraints
To comply with financial regulations and data privacy, the system is strictly prohibited from collecting, processing, or storing:
*   PAN (Permanent Account Number)
*   Aadhaar Card numbers
*   Bank Account numbers or IFSC codes
*   OTPs (One-Time Passwords)
*   User email addresses or phone numbers

### 4.4. UI/UX Minimum Requirements
*   A persistent, visible **Disclaimer**: `Disclaimer: Facts-only. No investment advice.`
*   A welcome message introducing capabilities.
*   Three static **example questions** to guide user interactions.
